Step-by-Step Simplex Method

  1. Convert the LP problem to standard form
  2. Identify pivot column (most negative in Z row)
  3. Identify pivot row (smallest positive ratio)
  4. Perform pivotal elimination and iterate
  5. Check for optimality or repeat steps
